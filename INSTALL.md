# Installing Fraxx – step by step

Fraxx is a **web app** (PWA). There is no installer to download: you open the address once in your browser and add the app from there. After that Fraxx has its own icon, opens without the browser bar and also works **offline**.

**Address:** https://jm-berlin.github.io/fraxx/

> Deutsche Fassung: [INSTALLATION.md](INSTALLATION.md) · PDF: [Fraxx-Install-Guide.pdf](Fraxx-Install-Guide.pdf)

---

## 1. Add the app

### iPhone and iPad (Safari)
1. Open **Safari** (not Chrome) and go to the address above.
2. Wait until the start page has fully loaded.
3. Tap the **Share icon** (square with an arrow pointing up). On iPhone it is at the bottom, on iPad at the top right.
4. Scroll down the list and tap **“Add to Home Screen”**.
5. The name “Fraxx” is already filled in. Tap **“Add”** at the top right.
6. The Fraxx icon appears on your home screen. From now on, start the app from there.

### Android (Chrome)
1. Open **Chrome** and go to the address above.
2. Often a prompt **“Install Fraxx”** appears at the bottom. Tap it and confirm with **“Install”**.
3. If no prompt appears: tap the **menu ⋮** at the top right → **“Install app”** (on some devices “Add to Home screen”).
4. The icon appears on your home screen and in the app drawer.

### Windows and Mac (Chrome or Microsoft Edge) – recommended on Mac
1. Open **Chrome** or **Edge** and go to the address above.
2. Click the **install icon** on the right of the address bar (a screen with an arrow or plus).
   - Alternatively in Chrome: **menu ⋮** → **“Cast, save and share”** → **“Install page as app …”**
   - In Edge: **menu …** → **“Apps”** → **“Install this site as an app”**
3. Confirm with **“Install”**.
4. Fraxx opens in its own window and appears in the Start menu (Windows) or in the Applications folder and Dock (Mac).

### Mac (Safari, macOS Sonoma or later)
This works too, but on Mac Safari only has the system voices. For the best pronunciation, use **Chrome** instead (see above and section 3).

1. Open the address in **Safari**.
2. Menu **“File”** → **“Add to Dock …”** → **“Add”**.

> **Firefox** cannot install web apps on desktop. You can still use Fraxx normally in the browser there, or use Chrome, Edge or Safari to install it.

---

## 2. First start

1. **Choose your source language:** If a window “Which language do you speak?” appears, tap your language. You can change it later under ⚙. If no window appears, Fraxx starts in German.
2. **Language packs:** On first start Fraxx downloads German, English, French, Italian and Spanish. Depending on your connection this takes a few seconds (about 2–3 MB in total). Please stay online meanwhile.
3. Done. From now on everything also works **without internet**: the table, pronunciation (using your device’s voices), quiz, grammar, menu and cheat sheet.

---

## 3. Better pronunciation

Fraxx speaks with the voices of your device or browser and automatically picks the best one it finds.

- **Mac and Windows:** **Chrome** additionally offers Google’s natural-sounding voices, **Edge** Microsoft’s “Natural” voices. Fraxx uses them automatically. They need an internet connection; offline the computer uses its own voices.
- **iPhone and iPad:** All browsers use the same voices here, so Chrome makes no difference. It sounds better with the high-quality voices: **Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices** → choose a language → download a voice marked **“Enhanced”** or **“Premium”**.
- **Mac (for travelling without internet):** **System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System voice → Manage Voices** → download an “Enhanced” or “Premium” voice for each language.
- **Android:** **Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech output** (on some devices under System → Languages) → under “Speech Services by Google”, download the voice data for your languages so it also works offline.

> **Tip:** Download the voices at home on Wi-Fi before you travel. Then Fraxx sounds good even without internet.

---

## 4. Add more languages

1. Tap **⚙** at the top → **“App, Sprachpakete & Sicherung”** (app, language packs & backup).
2. Under **Sprachpakete** you see all available languages. “Standard” ones are already installed, “optional” ones can be added.
3. Tap **“Laden”** (load) next to a language. It is immediately available in the table, quiz, grammar and menu.
4. **“Entfernen”** (remove) frees the storage again. Your own entries are kept.

---

## 5. Back up your own entries (recommended)

Your own entries and changes are stored **only on this device**. To take them to a new device:

1. ⚙ → **“App, Sprachpakete & Sicherung”** → **“Sicherung herunterladen”** (download backup).
2. Save the file, e.g. in iCloud, Google Drive or OneDrive.
3. On the other device: install Fraxx → ⚙ → **“Sicherung einlesen”** (restore backup) → choose the file.

Fraxx reminds you automatically: once you have entries of your own, a notice with **“Jetzt sichern”** (back up now) appears after 14 days without a backup (and every 30 days after that).

> **Important:** Clearing the normal browser cache is harmless. **Clearing “site data” / “cookies and other site data”**, however, also removes your own entries. Back up first!

---

## 6. Your own translations (coming soon)

The language packs already contain more than 6,400 ready-made translated entries. Automatically translating new words and sentences of your own is not yet enabled in this test version. This feature will come in a later version.

---

## 7. Updates

Updates arrive automatically: every time Fraxx starts with an internet connection, it checks for a new version or new language packs and downloads only what has changed. To check manually: ⚙ → **“Nach Updates suchen”** (check for updates).

---

## 8. Uninstall

- **iPhone/iPad:** long-press the icon → “Remove App”.
- **Android:** long-press the icon → “Uninstall”.
- **Windows/Mac (Chrome/Edge):** open the app → menu ⋮ at the top right → “Uninstall Fraxx”.

Download a backup first if you want to keep your own entries.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| No “Add to Home Screen” on iPhone | The page must be opened in **Safari**, not in Chrome or another app. |
| No install icon on the computer | Use Chrome or Edge. If Fraxx is already installed, the button says “Open in app” instead. |
| No or robotic pronunciation | No (good) voice is installed for that language. See section 3, “Better pronunciation”. |
| A language pack does not load | Check your internet connection and tap “Laden” again under ⚙. |
