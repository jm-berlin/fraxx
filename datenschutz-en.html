Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This is a translation for your convenience. Only the German version is legally binding.

In short

Fraxx needs no account, sets no cookies, does not track visitors and shows no ads. What you do in the app, your stars, learning progress and settings, stays on your device and is not sent to us.

2. Hosting

Fraxx is served via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open the site, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, date and time, the requested address, and browser and device information. This is needed to deliver the site and operate it securely. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a working and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. We do not receive any access logs ourselves. For details, see GitHub's privacy statement.

3. Storage on your device

So that Fraxx also works offline, the app stores data in your browser's storage (local storage, IndexedDB and service worker cache): the app itself, the language packs, your settings, your cheat sheet and your learning progress. This data does not leave your device. The storage is strictly necessary for the function you requested (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser or uninstalling the app. Under ⚙ → App, language packs & backup you can also back up your data yourself.

4. Fonts

The fonts are hosted on our own server. No connection to Google or other providers is made for them.

5. Read aloud

To read text aloud, Fraxx uses the speech output of your device or browser. Some browsers use their manufacturer's online voices for this (for example Google voices in Chrome); in that case the text to be read is sent to that manufacturer. We have no influence on this; the privacy terms of the respective browser manufacturer apply.

6. Voice input and speaking practice

When you tap a microphone (in the search field or in the quiz under “Speaking”), Fraxx uses your browser’s speech recognition. Only then does your browser ask for permission to use the microphone. Depending on the browser, what you say is sent to its manufacturer and converted into text there, for example to Google in Chrome or to Apple in Safari; some devices recognise speech themselves. We have no influence on this; the privacy terms of the respective browser manufacturer apply. Fraxx only receives the recognised text and uses it right away for the search or the comparison.

During speaking practice, Fraxx also records your voice so that you can listen to it and compare it with Fraxx’s pronunciation. This recording stays only in your browser’s memory, is neither saved nor transmitted, and is deleted when you move on to the next sentence.

7. Links to other services

If a voice is missing on your device, Fraxx offers a link to Google Translate. Only when you tap it is Google's page opened and the text sent there. Google is responsible for the processing there.

8. Contact

If you contact us by email or phone, we use your details only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed for that purpose.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing of your data, to data portability, and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). Just send us an email.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
